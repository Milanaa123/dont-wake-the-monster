# Living Pets — Personality System (v0.6)

Status: **source-implemented and compiled + pure Luau logic-tested**. Roblox
Studio visuals, multiplayer timing and DataStore persistence across real
servers have **not** been tested yet.

## Design goal

Every owned pet is an **individual**, not a fixed personality attached to a
species. Two players may both own Pip, the pastel kitten, yet one can be Kind
and another Grumpy. Zuzu is a second kitten species with different markings.
The 30 species all use this system.

Each pet receives its own seeded identity at unlock. Older save files
without personalities are migrated into reproducible owner-specific seeds.
The seed and the current needs are saved in the same player profile as the
existing animals, crops, recipes and currency.

## Personality factors

- **12 core temperaments:** Kind, Grumpy, Playful, Mischievous, Shy, Brave,
  Curious, Sleepy, Dramatic, Clingy, Proud and Wild.
- **12 persistent habits:** Cuddles, Dances, CollectsShiny, Naps, Hides,
  Jumps, MakesFaces, Hums, ChasesTail, StealsSnacks, Daydreams and Sneezes.
- **7 possible fears:** Thunder, Darkness, Crowds, SuddenNoise, BigMonsters,
  Water and None.
- **7 preferred forms of affection:** HeadPats, Cuddles, Dancing, Games,
  Food, QuietTime and Praise.
- **Food tastes:** one favorite and one different disliked option from Candy,
  Pepper, Bubble and Star.
- **6 fixed numerical traits (1–100 range):** kindness, curiosity, courage,
  mischief, sociability, sensitivity. Core temperament biases one trait; it
  does not eliminate other combinations.

These categorical factors alone yield a **theoretical design space of
84,672 different combinations** (12 × 12 × 7 × 7 × 4 × 3), before counting
trait values, changing moods, bond and needs. This is a design-space count,
**not** a claim that all of them have been observed in live Roblox play.

## Dynamic character state

- **Happiness**, **energy**, **hunger** and **bond** change through feeding,
  petting, games, calming the pet, social encounters and time.
- **9 mood labels**: Happy, Calm, Grumpy, Excited, Shy, Scared, Sleepy,
  Hungry and Curious. Moods are *derived* from needs, courage and context.
- **Friendship levels:** Just Met → Getting Close → Friends → Best Friends →
  Soulmates. Food tastes are hidden until trust reaches 25 points.
- The active creature follows the player and has mood-specific floating
  labels and temperament-based accessories. Some habits alter its idle
  movement (hiding, cuddling, jumping, dancing, tail-chasing).
- **Temperament changes the weighted chaos reactions** when pets are fed.
  Favorite food improves happiness/bond and lowers resulting noise.
  Disliked food reduces happiness and increases noise.
- A pet afraid of giant monsters can become visibly Scared on wakeup.
  Timed idle updates slowly change the creature's hunger and energy.

## Social behavior (multiplayer)

When two owners and their companions are close on a sleeping island,
a server-limited encounter may occur. There are **12 scene types**,
including Besties, Prank, DanceOff, DramaDuel, HideAndSeek,
NapTogether, SneezeChain, Singing, Zoomies, ShinyTrade, Rivalry and Cuddle.

The outcome is influenced by **both** pets' identities and temperaments:
two Sleepy creatures can nap together, a Mischievous creature can prank a
Grumpy one, and a Wild one may play hide-and-seek with a Shy one.
Encounters change happiness and may increase the shared noise gauge,
potentially waking the giant. No infinite coin reward is attached.

Only one encounter can be generated by the server every 25-second
personality tick. Nearby player roots and two existing pets are required.
Clients cannot directly claim social event rewards.

## How players interact

1. Open **MY PETS**: owned creatures show their individual temperament,
   current mood and bond percentage.
2. Open **PET DIARY**: see habits, fear, love language, six traits, four
   need meters and current friendship stage.
3. **PET**, **PLAY** or **SOOTHE** your pet. Requests are checked on the
   server for valid action, distance, cooldown and game phase.
4. Feed any of the normal or garden-crafted snacks: tastes change the
   result and teach the player what their specific pet enjoys.
5. Meet other players to trigger spontaneous social scenes.

## Current technical limitations

- **Not filmed or visually played inside Roblox Studio.** CI compiles/builds
  sources but cannot validate actual camera, animation, pathing or UI quality.
- The original DataStore profile still needs session-lock protection before
  taking money or launching publicly. No Robux products have been enabled.
- Each player owns one copy of a given species; this system personalizes
  each player's copy. Owning multiple differently-tempered cats *of the same
  species within the same account* would require an instance-based inventory
  in a later milestone.
- World accessories are primitive placeholder 3D shapes, not polished
  hand-made models or character skeletal animations.

## Source and verification

- `src/shared/PetPersonality.luau`: seeded identity, mood, food tastes,
  bonding, reaction multipliers, idle and social encounter rules.
- `src/server/Progression.luau`: migration, saving and safe catalog views.
- `src/server/Game.server.luau`: authoritative interactions and multiplayer.
- `src/server/World.luau`: mood badges, feline markings and accessories.
- `src/client/PetDiary.client.luau`: smartphone/desktop pet diary.
- `scripts/test_personality.py`: runs the actual pure Luau rules in GitHub
  Actions and verifies determinism, save restoration, tamper rejection,
  emotions, tastes, bonding and social variety.

All game actions and coins remain free-to-play in the prototype.
